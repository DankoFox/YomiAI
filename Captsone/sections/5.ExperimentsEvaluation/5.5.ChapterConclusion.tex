\section{Chapter Conclusion}

This chapter evaluated the proposed recommendation and search system across multiple dimensions.

Pipeline~A (Cleora + BGE-M3) achieves HR@10 = 0.9047 and Pipeline~B (DIF-SASRec) achieves HR@10 = 0.7745 on 100,000 held-out users with N = 99 sampled negatives.
Both substantially outperform the Content Baseline (0.4346) and the prior GRU-SeqDQN approach (0.1031).
The DIF-SASRec result is stable across five random evaluation seeds (sd = 0.0024), confirming it is not an artefact of a particular negative draw.

BGE-M3 was chosen over the legacy BLaIR encoder primarily for multilingual support: on long Vietnamese queries, BGE-M3 reaches Precision@10 = 0.55 while BLaIR returns 0.05.
The HNSW index reduces P50 FAISS latency from 612 ms to 120 ms at the cost of 47.9\% approximate recall, which the downstream BM25 re-ranking stage partially recovers.
Combined with encoder and parallelisation improvements, warm-cache end-to-end search latency drops from 1,102 ms to 59 ms.

The complementarity analysis shows the two pipelines cover largely non-overlapping cases: Pipeline~A alone serves 19.9\% of users that Pipeline~B misses, and Pipeline~B alone serves 6.9\% that Pipeline~A misses.
The 2.6\% of users neither pipeline reaches are mainly those with long-tail target items outside the Cleora index, very sparse histories, or recent cross-domain genre shifts.
