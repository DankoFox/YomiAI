\section{System Architecture and Methodology}
\label{sec:overview}

Figure~\ref{fig:arch} illustrates the dual-mode data flow.
The system is organised into two phases.
Phase~1 (\emph{representation learning})
encodes items and users into three complementary latent spaces:
a \emph{textual} space (BGE-M3 on item metadata),
a \emph{visual} space (CLIP on cover images), and
a \emph{behavioural} space (Cleora on the co-purchase interaction graph).
These three spaces are grouped into two sub-boxes in the diagram:
Item Content (textual + visual) and User Behavior (behavioural).

Phase~2 consumes these representations through two
independent pipelines that operate on the same shared profile.
\emph{Active Search} (Mode~1) handles user-initiated text or
image queries by encoding into the textual and visual spaces,
retrieving across three parallel channels
(BGE-M3 HNSW, Tantivy BM25, CLIP HNSW), and fusing results via
Adaptive Weighted RRF.
\emph{Recommendation} (Mode~2) generates proactive suggestions
through two sub-pipelines:
Pipeline~A retrieves behavioural candidates from the Cleora
co-purchase graph, passes them through a content-veto filter,
and presents the survivors to the user (People Also Buy);
Pipeline~B retrieves candidates by BGE-M3 HNSW semantic search,
applies the same content veto, and scores survivors with
DIF-SASRec (You Might Like).
Online interactions continuously update the Cleora
embeddings and fine-tune the per-user DIF-SASRec model.

\subsection{Data Layer}
The item catalog contains 3.08 million Amazon Books entries.
Text embeddings (1024-dim, BGE-M3~\cite{chen2024bgem3}) and image embeddings (512-dim, CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip}) are
indexed in HNSW-based approximate nearest-neighbor structures for dense retrieval.
A co-purchase behavioral graph embedding scheme (Cleora~\cite{cleora2021}) covers 375k items
with at least one co-purchase edge.
An inverted index\footnote{\url{https://github.com/quickwit-oss/tantivy}} supports BM25~\cite{robertson1994okapi} keyword retrieval.

\subsection{User Profile}
Each user maintains a persistent MongoDB document storing:
(i)~an aggregated content embedding (exponential temporal weighting,
half-life 30 interactions),
(ii)~a rolling 50-item interaction history for DIF-SASRec inference, and
(iii)~a raw interaction audit trail.
Profiles are updated asynchronously after every click with user-level locking
to prevent race conditions.
This shared representation feeds both pipelines in Mode~2
(Section~\ref{ssec:passive}): Pipeline~A uses the aggregated content embedding
for semantic re-ranking of behavioral candidates, and Pipeline~B uses the
rolling interaction history as input to DIF-SASRec.
