\chapter{API Endpoint Reference}
\label{app:api}

Table~\ref{tab:api_endpoints} documents the seven route groups exposed by the FastAPI backend, together with their HTTP method, path, and primary function.
All endpoints return JSON and are served at \texttt{http://localhost:8000} in the development configuration.

\endfoot
\hline
\endlastfoot
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Authentication}} \\
POST & \texttt{/auth/check}       & Check whether a user ID exists in MongoDB \\
POST & \texttt{/auth/create}      & Create a new user record and initialise an empty profile \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Search}} \\
POST & \texttt{/search}           & Multimodal query (text and/or image); returns top-$k$ items with RRF, BGE-M3, and CLIP score fields \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Recommendations}} \\
GET  & \texttt{/recommend}        & Dual-pipeline recommendations; returns \texttt{people\_also\_buy} (Pipeline~A) and \texttt{you\_might\_like} (Pipeline~B) with per-item score and layer fields \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Interaction logging}} \\
POST & \texttt{/interact}         & Log a click, skip, or not\_interested event; triggers a DIF-SASRec gradient step for click events only; not\_interested items are added to a per-user Redis blacklist \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{User profile}} \\
GET  & \texttt{/profile}          & Return click sequence, session statistics (CTR, train steps, interactions), and training loss history \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{LLM assistant}} \\
POST & \texttt{/ask\_llm\_stream} & Stream Qwen2.5 book description as SSE token chunks (\textbf{Genre}, \textbf{Plot Summary}, \textbf{Pitch}) \\
\hline
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Health check}} \\
GET  & \texttt{/health}           & Return service status and index loading state \\
\end{longtable}
